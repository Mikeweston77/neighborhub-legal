NeighborHub - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to NeighborHub ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access the App.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register for NeighborHub or use the App, we may collect:

  • Name: Your full name for account setup and community identification
  • Email Address: For account creation, login, and communication
  • Contact Number: For support, verification, and optional account recovery or emergency-related features
  • Address or Residence Details: To verify neighborhood residency and eligibility for community access
  • Profile Photo: If you choose to upload one
  • Verification Details: Information submitted or reviewed during neighborhood/admin verification

Location Information

We may collect GPS location data while you use the App to:

  • Display local weather information
  • Support neighborhood verification and local community features
  • Show nearby events, services, and business search results
  • Support safety-related and neighbourhood watch functionality where enabled
  • Live location during a patrol: while you are actively taking part in a neighbourhood patrol, your live location is shared with the other volunteers on that patrol and with admins and committee members, so participants can see each other and coordinate. Your live location entry is deleted when you leave the patrol or are removed from it.
  • Wellness check-in location: if you ask for help during a wellness check-in, the device location at that moment is included with your request so responders can find you.
  • Location Frequency: Only when you allow the App to access location while using it
  • You Control It: You can disable location services in iOS Settings

Content You Create

We also collect content you create in the App, including:

  • Messages: Community chat messages, replies, and reactions, and patrol chat messages sent during a patrol
  • Posts: Incident reports, event posts, marketplace listings, polls, and neighbourhood watch updates
  • Media: Photos, videos, voice messages, and documents you upload
  • Interactions: RSVPs, poll votes, business searches, and similar activity

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features you use, screens you view, and time spent in the App
  • Diagnostics: Crash reports, performance data, and error logs used for stability and troubleshooting

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. Provide Core Services

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify you're a neighborhood resident
  • Enable community chat and messaging
  • Process incident reports, events, marketplace listings, polls, and neighbourhood watch activity
  • Display local businesses, weather information, and nearby services

2. Personalization

  • Show relevant local weather and news
  • Recommend nearby businesses and local content
  • Customize your experience based on preferences

3. Communication

  • Send push notifications for messages, alerts, and updates
  • Email notifications for account activity
  • Emergency alerts and safety notifications

4. Safety and Security

  • Verify user identities
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Moderate content for community safety
  • Respond to emergency or safety-related requests where available

5. Analytics and Improvement

  • Understand how users engage with features
  • Identify and fix bugs
  • Improve app performance
  • Develop new features

6. Payments and Billing

  • Process membership contributions and other eligible payments
  • Support transaction records and billing-related support requests
  • Help prevent fraud and unauthorized transactions

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We Share Your Information With:

Other Neighborhood Members:

  • Your name and profile photo are visible to verified community members
  • Content you post (messages, events, listings) is visible to the community
  • Your precise location is not shown to the general community, and we never publish it to other users' maps or feeds

Safety and Wellness Features (only when you use them):

  • If you send a wellness check-in, a "need help" request, or a safety/emergency request, the details you provide — including your address, contact number, emergency contact, and where available your device's live location — are shared with the community admins and committee members who respond to those requests
  • Where a request is routed to specific neighbours or emergency contacts you have nominated, those recipients receive the same details so they can assist you
  • Neighbourhood-watch incident reports you submit may include the location you provide, visible to approved camera-access users, admins and committee members
  • Wellness check-ins require your separate, explicit consent before they can be enabled, and patrol location sharing requires its own consent before the patrol workspace opens. See Consent and Your Choices In the App below.

Within the App:

  • Content visible to verified community members remains visible within the App as intended
  • Neighborhood-watch and other community safety content may be shown to approved users according to app permissions

Service Providers:

  • Firebase (Google): Cloud hosting, authentication, database, storage, analytics and Cloud Functions
  • Google (Gemini API): AI assistant, AI search and writing suggestions — used only when you enable AI features
  • Apple: Push notifications (APNs)
  • OneSignal: Delivery of push notifications
  • PayStack: Payment processing for membership contributions and other eligible payments
  • OpenWeatherMap: Weather data (location coordinates only)

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for marketing outside the app

Required Disclosures:

We may disclose your information if required by:

  • Law enforcement or legal obligations
  • Court orders or subpoenas
  • Emergency situations involving safety or security

Consent and Your Choices In the App

Some NeighborHub features share information about you with other people. Those features are optional and remain off until you explicitly agree. Before each one is enabled, the App shows a written notice explaining what is collected, who can see it, how it is used, and how to withdraw — and nothing is shared unless you tap Agree.

  • AI features — the AI assistant, AI search and writing suggestions. Your input and recent in-app context are sent to our AI provider only after you accept the AI data sharing notice.
  • Wellness check-ins — daily check-ins together with the address, contact number, emergency contact and, on request, live location that go with them, shared with the people you select.
  • Neighbourhood patrol — your live location while you are on patrol, shared with the other volunteers on that patrol and with admins and committee members.

How we record your consent

When you agree, we record the acceptance against your account together with a server-generated timestamp, the version of the notice you accepted, and the platform. This lets us demonstrate that consent was given and, if you change your mind, that it was withdrawn. You can review the current state of each consent in the App at any time.

How to withdraw consent

  • AI: Settings, then AI & Privacy, turn AI data sharing off. All future AI requests stop.
  • Wellness: Settings, then Wellness, switch check-ins off. No further check-in data is sent.
  • Patrol: Watch Settings, then Patrol Location Sharing, tap Revoke consent. No further patrol location sharing can start. If you are currently on a patrol, leave it to stop sharing immediately.

Withdrawing consent does not remove records created while consent was in place (for example, patrols you already completed), and does not affect information we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

Neighbourhood Watch & Patrol

Patrol is a voluntary feature that lets neighbours organise watches. When you take part:

  • Live location: while you are actively on patrol, your location is shared with the other volunteers on the same patrol, who see it on the patrol map, and with admins and committee members. Residents who are not on your patrol cannot see it.
  • Deletion: your live location entry is deleted when you leave the patrol or are removed from it. It is a current position, not a stored location history.
  • Patrol chat: messages you send in the patrol chat are visible to the participants of that patrol and to admins and committee members.
  • Participation records: we keep a record of which patrols you joined and whether they were completed, so that patrols can be organised and reported on.
  • Notifications: patrol alerts are delivered to your device. You can turn them off in Watch Settings.

Patrol location sharing requires your separate consent, described above.

Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: We retain your data while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: When you delete your account, we remove or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, subject to legal and operational requirements
  • Content: Messages and posts you created may remain visible in an anonymized form (for example, as "[Deleted User]")
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes

Your Rights and Choices

You Have the Right To:

1. Access: Request a copy of your personal data

2. Correction: Update or correct your information in Settings

3. Deletion: Delete your account and associated data

4. Opt-Out: Disable push notifications, location services, or analytics

How to Exercise Your Rights:

  • Update Profile: Go to Settings → Edit Profile
  • Delete Account: Use the home tab user settings to delete your account, or contact support for assistance
  • Contact Us: Send email to neighbourhubapp@gmail.com

Location Services:

  • iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services → NeighborHub
  • Choose: "Never," "Ask Next Time," or "While Using the App"

Push Notifications:

  • iOS Settings → Notifications → NeighborHub
  • Toggle off specific notification types

Payments

Membership contributions and other eligible payments are processed through PayStack. We may share necessary transaction information with PayStack to complete payment processing, prevent fraud, and support billing-related inquiries. We do not directly store full card details on our servers.

Diagnostics and Analytics

We use analytics and crash reporting to understand how the App is used, improve reliability, diagnose issues, and develop new features. This may include feature usage data, performance information, and error reports.

Children's Privacy

NeighborHub is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication
  • Firestore Security Rules: Database access controls
  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Storage Security: Cloud Storage access restrictions
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security reviews

However, no system is 100% secure. Use strong passwords and keep your login credentials confidential.

AI Features and Your Data

NeighborHub includes optional AI features (the AI assistant, AI search and writing suggestions). These are powered by a third-party AI provider, Google (Gemini API), and are used only when you explicitly opt in.

  • What is sent: the text you type into an AI feature, recent in-app context used to answer you, your device locale and time zone, and your app user identifier used to authenticate the request
  • What is never sent: your name, address, contact number, emergency contacts and wellness check-in data
  • How to withdraw: Settings → AI & Privacy lets you turn AI data sharing off at any time, which stops all future AI requests
