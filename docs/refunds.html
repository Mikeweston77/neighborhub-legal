NeighborHub - Refunds & Cancellation

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the monthly NeighborHub community membership contribution is billed, cancelled and refunded. The contribution is collected on behalf of the residents' committee for shared neighbourhood services, and card payments are processed by Paystack.

1. Billing

  • The membership contribution is charged monthly in advance by card through Paystack.
  • The amount and currency are shown on the checkout page before you pay, and on the receipt emailed to you after payment.
  • NeighborHub does not store your card details. Paystack is the merchant of record for the card transaction.
  • Membership is a community contribution: it unlocks nothing in the app, and every verified resident keeps full access either way.

2. Cancelling

  • You may cancel at any time by emailing neighbourhubapp@gmail.com from the address registered to your account, or from the membership screen in the app.
  • Cancellation stops the next monthly charge. It does not refund the current month, which remains active until the end of the paid period.
  • Your access to the community ends when the paid period ends. Content you posted remains visible to the community as described in our Privacy Policy.

3. When we refund

We will refund a payment in full where:

  • you were charged twice for the same household in the same month;
  • you were charged in error, or after a cancellation you had already requested;
  • the payment was made for the wrong household and cannot be transferred;
  • a payment succeeded but the service was not made available to you and we cannot fix it within 7 days; or
  • a refund is required by applicable law.

Refund requests must be made within 7 days of the charge.

4. When we do not refund

  • Months of membership that have already been used.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after the charge.
  • Contributions already allocated by the residents' committee to services delivered for that month.
  • Chargebacks raised without first contacting us — please contact us first so we can resolve it directly.

5. How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are returned to the original card through Paystack. We cannot refund to a different card or to cash.
  • We process approved refunds within 3 business days. Your bank then takes a further 5 to 10 business days to reflect the credit.
  • You will receive an email confirming the refund and its Paystack reference.

6. Failed or disputed payments

If a payment fails, no charge is made and you can retry. If a payment is disputed with the card issuer, we will provide Paystack with the transaction record, and access may be suspended while the dispute is open.

7. Contact

Refund and cancellation requests go to the NeighborHub support address below. Include the email address registered to your account, the payment date, and the reference shown on your Paystack receipt.
